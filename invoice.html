Invoice generator

Home page

A dashboard overview displaying key business insights, including a monthly revenue graph, the top 3 clients based on revenue, the 3 most recent invoices, and other relevant statistics. This ensures a quick and clear snapshot of performance at a glance.

Invoice Creation Page

Generate professional invoices by entering key details such as the client's name, invoice date, hourly rate, and total hours worked. The form ensures structured data entry for consistent and accurate invoice generation.

Invoice Overview

A centralized page displaying all created invoices, sorted by date. Users can easily browse, search, and manage past invoices, allowing for quick access and streamlined financial tracking.

Invoice PDF Export

Each invoice is automatically converted into a clean, easy-to-read PDF format, ready for direct download or client distribution. The layout is intentionally minimalistic to ensure clarity and professionalism.

Technologies Used

Stack: Python, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Django and Docker.
